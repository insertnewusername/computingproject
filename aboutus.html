About Project Cyber and its Creators

Making cybersecurity a priority through engaging games

Who are we?

We are Project Cyber, a 3 man team of commited developers, attempting to teach cyber security to all the gamers out there. Alternatively to reading boring manuals or watching dry videos, we have comprised the ultimate package of 3 games to teach you how to be safe online in an engaging format.

Our Mission

To make cybersecurity a second nature to all of those who use oline platforms on a day-to-day basis, ensuring accessibilty, enjoyability, and a memorable experience through immersive gameplay.

Our Audience

Students, families and anyone who wants to broaden their knowledge through fun and interactive learning.

Our Vision

A world where everyone is knowledgable enough on the topic of cybersecurity to understand when they are encountering a threat and how to manage said threat.
